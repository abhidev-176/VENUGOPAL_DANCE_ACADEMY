Founder & Head Instructor

Abhinay — Founder & Head Instructor


Hi, I'm the founder of Abhi Dance Academy. My journey with dance began over a decade ago, and this academy is my way of sharing that passion with the community. "Move. Express. Inspire." isn't just our tagline — it's what I try to bring into every class I teach.


Our mission is to make dance accessible to everyone — building discipline, confidence, and artistry in every student, regardless of age or experience. We don't just teach dance, we build character.

Meet Our Teachers

Meera Krishnan

Bharatanatyam Instructor

Meera has trained in classical Bharatanatyam for over 15 years and has performed at numerous temple festivals and cultural showcases. She focuses on precise footwork, expressive hand gestures (mudras), and storytelling through movement, helping students connect with the emotional depth of classical Indian dance while building strong technical fundamentals from day one.

Rahul Verma

Hip-Hop Instructor

Rahul grew up battling in street dance crews before transitioning into teaching. His classes are high-energy and focus on groove, isolations, freestyle, and choreography that translates from the studio to real competitions. He's known for pushing students to find their own style rather than just copying steps.

Arjun Nair

Folk Dance Instructor

Arjun specializes in regional folk dance forms passed down through generations in his family. His classes cover Bhangra, Garba, and other traditional styles, blending high-energy group choreography with cultural storytelling so students understand the roots of every step, not just the movement itself.

Vikram Iyer

Kids Dance Instructor

Vikram specializes in working with our youngest students, ages 5 to 10. His classes focus on building rhythm, coordination, and confidence through playful choreography and simple routines, creating a fun, encouraging environment where kids fall in love with dance before technical training gets serious.

Sanya Kapoor

Contemporary & Bollywood Instructor

Sanya trained in contemporary dance and jazz-funk before specializing in Bollywood choreography. Her classes emphasize fluid movement, emotional expression, and stage presence, and she regularly choreographs the academy's showcase performances and competition routines.
