Achievements

Some of the milestones our teams and students have hit over the years.

State-Level Folk Dance Championship — 1st Place

Our Folk Dance team, trained under Arjun Nair, won first place at the State-Level Folk Dance Championship with a high-energy group routine blending Bhangra and Garba formations.

Category: Folk Dance (Group) · Team: Abhi Folk Ensemble

Regional Street Dance Battle — Winners

Our senior Hip-Hop crew, coached by Rahul Verma, took the top spot at a regional street dance battle, competing against over 15 crews from across the region.

Category: Hip-Hop (Crew Battle) · Team: Abhi Hip-Hop Crew

Inter-Academy Classical Dance Meet — 2nd Place

Students from our Bharatanatyam batch, trained by Meera Krishnan, placed second at an inter-academy classical dance meet judged on technique, expression, and choreography.

Category: Classical Dance (Solo & Group) · Team: Abhi Classical Wing

Annual Academy Trophy Shelf

Over the years, our students and teams have brought home multiple trophies across dance styles — a small snapshot of the hard work put in during rehearsals and showcases.

Multiple categories · 15+ awards to date

Youth Dance Fest — Best Choreography Award

Our Contemporary & Bollywood batch, choreographed by Sanya Kapoor, won Best Choreography at a youth dance fest for an original fusion piece blending both styles.

Category: Contemporary / Bollywood Fusion · Team: Abhi Contemporary Wing
