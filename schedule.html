How Batches Work

Each class runs as a 12-week batch, after which students can either continue into the next level or switch styles. Beginners start in the fundamentals batch for their chosen style, focusing on posture, basic steps, and rhythm before moving into choreography-heavy intermediate batches.

Trial Classes

New students are welcome to attend one free trial class in any style before enrolling, so they can get a feel for the instructor's teaching style and the energy of the batch before committing.

Makeup Classes

If a student misses a class due to illness or a scheduling conflict, they can attend a makeup session in a different batch of the same style during the same week, subject to space availability.

Holiday Schedule

Classes follow a standard academic calendar and pause for major public holidays. Any schedule changes are communicated to students in advance through our contact channels, and make-up sessions are typically added the following week.

Performance Prep Season

In the lead-up to our annual showcase (usually held in December) and major competitions, select batches move to extended rehearsal timings on weekends. These are announced separately and are optional for students not participating in that year's performance.
