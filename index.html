About The Academy

Abhi Dance Academy was founded on a simple belief: dance is one of the most powerful ways to build confidence, discipline, and joy in a person's life. What started as a handful of students practicing in a small hall has grown into a full academy offering classical, urban, and contemporary dance forms to students of every age.

We don't just teach steps. Every class is built around technique, musicality, and expression, so students walk away not just knowing choreography but genuinely understanding the art form they're learning. Whether a student wants to perform on stage, compete, or simply dance for the love of it, we build a program around their goals.

Our instructors come from diverse dance backgrounds — from classical Indian dance trained under respected gurus, to hip-hop dancers who've performed in street battles and stage shows, to contemporary and folk specialists who bring their own regional traditions into the studio. That diversity is what makes our teaching well-rounded.

Our Philosophy

Move. Express. Inspire. That's the core of what we do here — we don't just build dancers, we build performers who understand rhythm, storytelling, and stage presence. Every batch, regardless of style, is trained to think about why a move is made, not just how.

Who Can Join

No prior experience needed. We welcome absolute beginners as young as 5 years old, as well as adults picking up dance for the first time, and experienced dancers looking to specialize further or prep for competitions.
