Our Classes

Bharatanatyam

Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest classical dance forms of India, originating in the temples of Tamil Nadu. It combines precise footwork, expressive hand gestures called mudras, and dramatic facial expressions to tell stories from mythology and everyday life. Students learn the fundamentals of adavus (basic steps), rhythm, and posture before progressing to full choreographed pieces. This class builds incredible discipline, flexibility, and body control, and is a great foundation for any other dance style.

Hip-Hop

Our Hip-Hop classes cover the high-energy urban styles that originated on the streets — breaking, popping, locking, and freestyle. Students learn sharp isolations, groove, and musicality while building the stamina and confidence needed to perform choreographed routines or battle freestyle. Classes are fast-paced and fun, blending fitness with self-expression. This is a great option for teens and adults who want an energetic workout alongside creative movement.

Contemporary

Contemporary dance blends elements of ballet, jazz, and modern dance into fluid, expressive movement. It emphasizes emotional storytelling over rigid technique, encouraging students to explore floor work, controlled falls, and dynamic transitions between positions. This class is ideal for students who want to develop versatility and artistic expression, and it pairs well with any other style since it strengthens core control and body awareness.

Bollywood

Bollywood dance is a vibrant fusion style inspired by Indian cinema, blending classical, folk, and modern movements into upbeat, expressive choreography. Classes focus on high-energy group routines set to popular Bollywood music, with emphasis on facial expression, hand movements, and stage presence. It's one of our most popular classes because it's fun, social, and accessible to complete beginners while still offering room to grow technically.

Kids Dance (Ages 5–10)

Our Kids Dance program introduces young children to rhythm, coordination, and basic choreography in a fun, encouraging environment. Classes mix simple steps from multiple styles, musical games, and creative movement exercises designed to build confidence rather than pressure technical perfection. It's often a child's first structured activity, and we focus heavily on making it enjoyable so they develop a genuine love for dance early on.

Folk Dance

Our Folk Dance classes celebrate regional traditions from across India, including Bhangra, Garba, and other high-energy group dances. Students learn traditional formations, rhythmic footwork, and the cultural context behind each dance form, often performed with props like sticks (dandiya) or to live drumming. These classes are lively, social, and a great way to connect with cultural heritage while getting a solid cardio workout.
